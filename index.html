 Who Am I?



Greetings! My name is Arjun Singh and I am a senior at North Central College majoring in graphic designer.

I mainly specialize in character design, but I also dabble a bit in posters designs, animations, and photography. I have been passionate in art ever since childhood with the goal of making my own story to share with the world.

In my free time, I enjoy hanging out with friends, collecting figures, listening to music, and playing the violin.
